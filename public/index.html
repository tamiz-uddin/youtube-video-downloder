Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download TikTok videos without a watermark?

Copy the share link of the TikTok video, paste it into the VidLoader search box, and click Fetch. VidLoader accesses the underlying CDN video stream directly, delivering the raw MP4 video without the TikTok watermark logo.

Does VidLoader merge separate video and audio tracks for 1080p?

Yes. Many platforms (like YouTube and Reddit) serve 1080p video and audio as separate adaptive streams. VidLoader utilizes an internal FFmpeg processing engine to mux the streams together into a universal MP4 file before saving.

Can I use VidLoader on iPhone, Android, or Mac?

VidLoader is built with progressive web app (PWA) technology. You can tap the Install button or add it to your home screen on iOS Safari, Android Chrome, Windows, and Mac for an app-like experience with no installation required.

Is VidLoader free and secure?

Yes, VidLoader is 100% free with no account registration. The server operates in an ephemeral, zero-log mode with real-time SSRF protection, strict rate limiting, and automated cleanup to guarantee complete user privacy.
